💡 Phase 12 • 5 Data-Driven Business Insights

1. Geographic Demand & Yield Concentration Finding: DHA generates 38.0% of total bookings and 42.2% of realized revenue (₨15,388).
Business Meaning: DHA is the platform's prime revenue engine. Protecting driver supply here is essential.
2. Revenue Mirage vs True Reliability Finding: DRV-002 earned highest revenue (₨4,484) but suffered cancellations. DRV-001, 004, 010 achieved 100% completion (>₨4,000).
Business Meaning: Pure revenue incentives encourage trip cherry-picking; multi-criteria ranking rewards reliable partners.
3. Gulberg Churn & Driver Supply Mismatch Finding: Gulberg booked 20 rides but had 6 cancellations (30.0% churn) and the lowest avg fare (₨397.07).
Business Meaning: Traffic and lower fares cause driver rejections, leading to severe passenger churn.
4. Premium Tier Superior Unit Economics Finding: Premium trips average ₨558.05 (+30.3% over Standard) across similar travel distances (13.5 vs 12.5 km).
Business Meaning: High passenger willingness-to-pay makes Premium expansion the fastest path to margin growth.
5. Digital Ecosystem vs Cash Dominance Finding: Cash generates 38.7% of revenue (₨14,115), but digital channels collectively command 61.3% (₨22,351).
Business Meaning: Heavy cash reliance creates operational disputes; digital onboarding accelerates trip turnover.

🚀 Phase 13 • 3 Strategic Actions & Architecture Note

Action 1: Geofenced Fulfillment Boost for Gulberg Implement a ₨50 - ₨75 driver incentive for completed Gulberg pickups during high-demand days (Thursday & Saturday).
Expected Impact: Drops Gulberg churn from 30% to <12%, recovering ~₨1,800 in lost daily bookings.
Action 2: Multi-Factor Driver Priority Allocation Reward drivers with ≥90% completion and ≥4.3 ★ rating (DRV-001, 004, 010) with priority dispatch for Premium trips.
Expected Impact: Eliminates passenger abandonment and increases top-tier driver retention.
Action 3: 5% Instant Digital Settlement Incentive Offer 5% fare cashback on Card, UPI, and Wallet settlements to migrate cash volume.
Expected Impact: Reduces cash share from 38.7% to <20%, curbing cash reconciliation friction.
