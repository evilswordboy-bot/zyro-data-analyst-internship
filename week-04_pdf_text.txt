Week 4: Power BI Dashboard Development 
 
Project Progress 
Week 2: We analyzed ride demand by date, month, hour, weekday/weekend, location, and customer behavior. 
Week 3: We analyzed revenue by different dimensions and evaluated driver performance using important KPIs. 
Current Stage: We now turn these analysis results into an interactive Power BI dashboard that presents the 
project's key findings clearly. 
 
1. Understand the Business Problem 
Raw analysis is useful, but a business needs a quick visual overview of its performance. 
In Week 4, your task is to convert the findings from Week 2 and Week 3 into a simple and interactive dashboard. 
The dashboard should help answer: 
• How many rides are happening?  
• How much revenue is generated?  
• When is ride demand highest?  
• Which locations are busiest?  
• How are drivers performing?  
• What important patterns can the business see?

11. Dashboard Layout

Top Section 
• KPI Cards  
Middle Section 
• Ride Demand  
• Peak Hours

Lower Section 
• Revenue

• Driver Performance  
Keep the dashboard clean and avoid putting too many charts on one page. 
 
12. Visualization Tasks 
Use the correct visual for each purpose: 
• Line Chart: Trends over time  
• Bar Chart: Category comparisons  
• Column Chart: Time/category comparisons  
• Card: Important single-number KPI  
• Table: Detailed driver/customer information  
Do not add charts just for decoration. 
Every visual should answer a business question. 
 
13. Business Questions

Your dashboard should help answer: 
1. When is ride demand highest?  
2. Which pickup locations are busiest?  
3. Which drop-off locations are most common?  
4. Which days have the highest demand?  
5. How much revenue was generated?

10. Are repeat customers an important part of the business? 
 
14. Business Insights 
Write at least 5 insights based on your dashboard. 
Each insight should explain what you found. 
Example: 
Evening hours have the highest ride demand, indicating that more driver availability may be required during this 
period. 
Another example: 
A small number of pickup locations contribute a large portion of total rides. 
Important: These are examples only. Use your actual dataset values 
 
15. Business Recommendations

Every recommendation should be connected to a finding from your dashboard
